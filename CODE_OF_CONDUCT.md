# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists to help people find work. We want taking part in it — asking
a question, reporting a bug, sending a patch — to be worth someone's time
regardless of who they are.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Assuming the other person is acting in good faith, and asking before assuming
  otherwise.
- Giving and accepting review on the work, not the person.
- Saying plainly when you were wrong. This project's history is full of it.

Behaviour that will not be accepted:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing someone's private information — a physical or email address, an
  employer, an application they made — without their explicit permission.
- Anything a reasonable professional would call harassment.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits) and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through GitHub's [private vulnerability
reporting](https://github.com/averatec0773/offeros/security/advisories/new) form,
which reaches the maintainer directly and is not publicly visible. Reports are
handled confidentially; the reporter's identity is not shared with the person
reported.

The maintainer will respond as follows, using judgement about severity:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.**
